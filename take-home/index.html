Step 6 of 6: Take it home. On your date, or any time after.

Take it home

On your date your bitcoin is yours again. You move it back to your own address, and nobody else is involved or can stop you. Bitcoin that went into a purchase has gone to the seller. The rest is yours.

Not open for real bitcoin yet

This page works on the test network only, for now. A real-bitcoin lock made in the Crowdlock app is taken home in the app.

Try it on the test network
Test network

This runs on a test version of bitcoin. Its coins are free, worthless and cannot be bought. Use it to find out how this works.

With a Ledger, on this page

Connect the Ledger you locked with. This page lists its locks, and any whose date has passed has a Take it home button. The Ledger shows you the payment and you approve it. The bitcoin goes back to your Ledger's own address.

Only locks on the register show here

A lock that was never published will not appear in this list. The date is judged by the bitcoin network's own clock, which runs about an hour behind real time on real bitcoin.

This browser cannot reach a Ledger. It needs Chrome, Edge or Brave on a computer. Use the Crowdlock app, or come back on a computer.

Change the fee

If a message here mentions an xpub: that is a public key a Ledger needs to see before it will sign. It is not secret.

Other ways home

The Crowdlock app

If you locked in the app, take it home in the app. It keeps its own list of your locks and shows the ones that are ready.

Other wallets

Not tested. Nobody has yet shown that Sparrow, Electrum or others can sign to take a lock home. What has been proved.

Good to know

  • Nothing happens by itself on the date. Your bitcoin stays where it is until you move it.
  • After its date a lock stops being counted. To stay in, lock again at step 4.
  • There is no deadline. Take it home on the date or years later.
  • You pay a network fee. It comes out of the amount, and the Ledger shows it before you approve.
